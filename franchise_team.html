Handover Checklist (reference)
Required items before marking a lead "Launched"
  • ✅ Token payment received & verified
  • ✅ Full investment paid & receipts shared
  • ✅ Franchise agreement signed by both parties
  • ✅ Site finalised & lease/rental documents shared
  • ✅ Interior & fit-out completed (per model spec)
  • ✅ Equipment delivered & installed
  • ✅ Staff hired & trained at HQ
  • ✅ Outlet record created in franchise_outlets table
  • ✅ Launch date confirmed with MF & Admin
