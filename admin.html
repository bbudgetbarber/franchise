Create Sales Head Login
Only the Admin can create Sales Heads. Each Sales Head can then create their own Sales Executives from within the Team Portal.
New Sales Head Account
Sales Heads see all leads in their assigned region and can route them to executives.
📌 How the team hierarchy works
  • One Sales Head per region (created here by Admin).
  • Each Head adds Sales Executives from their portal — they're auto-scoped to the Head's region.
  • Heads can assign / reassign any unassigned lead in their region to any executive.
  • Executives only see leads where assigned_team_id = themselves.
  • WhatsApp follow-ups are stage-aware and trigger the lead toward the final token payment.
